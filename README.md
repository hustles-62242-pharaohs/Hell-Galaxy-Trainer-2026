## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Fly through the Neterun galaxy, use normal maneuvering to position the bio-ship, and activate the boost when you need additional thrust. The Unlimited Boost option is useful for extended travel and evasive movement, but disabling it before entering menus or loading areas can improve stability.

### Tools & Inventory

HELL GALAXY focuses on gathered resources, ammunition, credits, weapons, and ship modules rather than a conventional character inventory. Collect materials while exploring ruins and stations, then use them for research, installation, repairs, and ship customization. Enable Unlimited Resources or Unlimited Credits before making purchases or installing modules.

### World & Environment

Explore Neterun's hostile regions, ancient alien ruins, abandoned locations, and mission areas. Search the environment for resources, follow quests, encounter rival raiders, and uncover the history of other exiles. Trainer options can reduce combat pressure while you investigate optional locations and experiment with different ship configurations.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat damages both the bio-ship and the pilot's mind. Switch between balanced, aggressive, and defensive focus states to respond to different threats. Conventional enemies pressure hull integrity, while ghostly creatures can attack sanity. Use Unlimited Hull Integrity and Unlimited Sanity when testing weapons or studying enemy behavior, and activate Unlimited Ammunition or No Weapon Cooldowns for uninterrupted combat.
